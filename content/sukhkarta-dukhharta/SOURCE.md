# सुखकर्ता दुःखहर्ता — SOURCE.md

- **Title (Marathi):** सुखकर्ता दुःखहर्ता
- **Deity:** Ganesha (श्री गणपती)
- **Author:** Samarth Ramdas (1608–1681/82). Signature "दास रामाचा" in the last stanza.
- **Language:** Marathi
- **Stanzas:** 3 numbered stanzas + 1 refrain (ध्रुवपद, "जय देव जय देव जय मंगलमूर्ती")

## Sources compared

1. Maharashtraweb — Ganpati Aarti in Marathi (Sukhkarta Dukhharta, attributed to Samarth Ramdas): https://www.maharashtraweb.com/prayers/ganpati-aarti
2. Shubhdarshika — Siddhatek Siddhivinayak page, full text: https://www.shubhdarshika.in/mr/temple/siddhatek-siddhivinayak

## Variant readings found and decision

- "नुरवी पुरवी" (both compared sources; some lyric sites give "नूरवी पूर्वी") — kept "नुरवी पुरवी".
- "कुंकुमकेशरा" (Maharashtraweb; some collections agree "कुंकुमकेशरा") — kept.
- "हिरेजडित मुकुट" (Maharashtraweb; some collections spell it "हिरे जडित") — kept "हिरेजडित", spelling only.
- "फणिवरबंधना" (Maharashtraweb) vs "फणीवरबंधना" (some collections) — kept "फणिवरबंधना"; a spelling variant only.
- "संकटी पावावे निर्वाणी रक्षावे" — both compared sources agree.
