# Madhyan Aarti (Sai Baba, midday) — SOURCE

- Work: Madhyan / Madhyahna Aarti (माध्यान्ह आरती), midday aarti of Shirdi Sai Baba,
  sung 12:00 noon
- Deity: Shirdi Sai Baba (Sadguru Sainath)
- Status: **INCLUDED — PARTIAL.** Only the parts below are included. The
  Namaskarashtak (Mohani Raj) sung in some lists is left out (see "Left out" below)
  because its author cannot be verified. Part order follows the Udupi temple
  listing, except that the Guru Prasada Yachana (Deo) is placed after the Mahimna
  Stotra, as in the Dhoop ordering — the order itself varies between sources, and
  the variant is recorded below.
- Licence for this text: PD-marked originals (see per-part verdicts). No modern
  translation, commentary, recording, or booklet layout is included.
- Proofing: **NEEDED.** Text written from the wordings compared below; it has not
  had independent Marathi proofreading. Report errors via the site's
  "Report an error" link.
- Disclaimer: attributions are traditional, taken from the parts' own signatures
  and from the Sagunopasana tradition; where two traditions exist both are
  recorded. We are not fighting for authority — the text is given as commonly sung.

Author-date evidence (checked live 2026-11-17/18):
- Adkar b. 5 Sep 1877, d. 18 Nov 1955; aarti written 1903–1904 —
  saiamrithadhara.com, "Madhavrao Adkar" (from a personal interview with his
  grandson Dr. Arun Adkar, *Shri Sai Leela* magazine, and *Sai Ki Anubhuti*
  Jul–Sep 2015); corroborated by *Shri Sai Leela* Sep–Oct 2012 (sai.org.in PDF:
  "On 18th November, 1955 he set for his heavenly abode from village Loni") and
  by the biography *Aarati Sai Baba Rachanakar Madhavrao Vamanrao Adkar*
  (Sterling, 2022).
- Bhishma b. 16 Aug 1854, d. 8 Aug 1935 — saiamrithadhara.com, "Krishnashastri
  Jageshwar Bhishma"; *Sagunopasana* history — "How K. J. Bhishma Composed Shri
  Sadguru Sainath Sagunopasana" (V. B. Kher, from *Shri Sai Leela*, Sep 1985),
  hosted by Shirdi Sai Trust: Khaparde published the booklet till 1922; the
  Sansthan published a revised edition from 1923 with Bhishma's consent.
- Dasganu b. 6 Jan 1868, d. 26 Nov 1962 — saiamrithadhara.com, "Das Ganu"
  (samadhi 26 Nov 1962, Pandharpur); the 1963 date appears in some blogs and in a
  scholarly chapter. Both dates are ≤1965, so the PD verdict does not turn on
  which is right; both are recorded here.
- Upasani Baba (Kashinath Govindrao Upasani), 15 May 1870 – 24 Dec 1941 —
  Wikipedia / Wikiwand, "Upasani Maharaj", and saiamrithadhara.com, "Upasani Baba".
- B. V. Deo (Balkrishna Vishwanath Deo), Mamlatdar of Dahanu, first visited
  Shirdi 30 Nov 1910, d. 1 Jan 1946 — saiamrithadhara.com, "Balasaheb Deo"
  (sources cited there: *Shri Sai Satcharita* Ch. 40–41 and *Shri Sai Leela*
  Magazine 1948 issue; grandson Madhavji Deo). He wrote under the pseudonym
  "Babache Bal" and composed an aarti song; the Guru Prasada Yachana is the
  piece credited to "B. V. Deo" in every aarti list checked.

## Left out — and why

- **Namaskarashtak — अनंता तुला तें कसें रे स्तवावें**, credited to
  "Shri Mohani Raj" (signature "भवीं मोहनीराजा" in the last verse). No
  biographical trace of Mohani Raj was found in any source checked — no birth or
  death year, no life, no first-publication date. The piece is one of the 16
  Sai-specific psalms in the Sagunopasana account, which shows it existed by the
  time of the booklet's formation (c. 1908–1923), but the edition in which it
  first appeared could not be established, so neither life+60 nor pre-1930
  publication can be verified. **Left out as unverifiable.** It can be revisited
  if a death year ≤1965, or a dated pre-1930 *Sagunopasana* edition containing it,
  is located. Its position (between Mantra Pushpam and Aisa Yei Ba in the Udupi
  order; between Naman and Aisa Yei Ba in the Dhoop order) is noted here so a
  singer can see where it belongs.

## Variant readings and ordering noted

- Part order: Udupi (B) orders the Madhyan as Panch-arati, Adkar, Jaya Deva,
  Dasganu, Naman, Mantra Pushpam, Namaskarashtak, Aisa Yei Ba, Mahimna. Other
  lists (e.g. Lyrics Nest, Shri Sai Parabrahma Mission) order it Naman,
  Namaskarashtak, Aisa Yei Ba, Mahimna, Guru Prasada, Mantra Pushpam. This file
  follows Udupi for parts 1–8 and places Guru Prasada after the Mahimna (as in
  the Dhoop lists); the variant is recorded here.
- Part 2 (Adkar): A prints the refrain again at the end of the aarti; it is
  printed once at the head here. "जाळुनियां अनंग" vs "जालुनिय अनङ्ग" is a
  spelling variant only.
- Part 5 (Naman): Udupi and A agree on the five blocks; A calls the Hare Rama
  section "नाम स्मरणं" (part 4 in its Dhoop numbering). "श्री गुरुदेव दत्त"
  follows the mantra in Udupi; kept here as the closing line of v. 5.
- Part 7 (Aisa Yei Ba): "बाबा येई बा" (C, Udupi) vs "(बाबाये) महाराजे ईबा"
  (A). The "बाबा येई बा" reading is used; the variant is recorded here.
- Part 8 (Mahimna): Udupi prints the Karacharana verse and "जय जय करुणाब्धे"
  at the end of the Mahimna; A (Dhoop) prints them after the Mantra Pushpam
  instead. This file follows Udupi (they close the Mahimna here), and the Dhoop
  file follows A — the same verses therefore appear in different positions in
  the two files, which is a genuine ordering variant, not a duplication error.
- Part 9, v. 9: A prints the "विमूढ म्हणुनि हसो … प्रपंचि मनहे रुसो" block as
  one continuous verse; kept as one numbered verse here.

## Sansthan position (checked live 2026-11-17/18)

- Shri Saibaba Sansthan Trust, Shirdi, official site (sai.org.in, Copyright page):
  "This web site is Copyright by the Shri Saibaba Sansthan Trust (Shirdi). All
  Rights including Copy Rights Reserved… No artwork, images or articles may be
  copied to another web site without prior written permission." This is a claim
  over the **website's own content**, not a claim over the underlying traditional
  aarti texts.
- The Sansthan states the copyright of the *Shri Sai Satcharita* book belongs to
  it and requires permission to reproduce it. That claim covers the Satcharita
  (a different work, not included here), not these aarti texts.
- The Sansthan publishes a revised edition of Bhishma's *Sagunopasana* since
  1923 and calls it the official Book of Daily Worship; that is a claim over its
  own edition/compilation and over its recordings and live-darshan broadcasts.
  No live Sansthan claim was found covering the underlying PD texts themselves.
- Conclusion applied here: the underlying texts by authors dead ≤1965 are
  republished from PD sources as above; no Sansthan booklet text, layout,
  translation, or recording is copied. If the Sansthan asserts a claim over a
  specific text here, that part will be removed and the claim recorded.
