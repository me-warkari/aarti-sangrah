# घालीन लोटांगण — SOURCE.md

- **Title (Marathi):** घालीन लोटांगण
- **Deity:** Closing prayer, no single deity
- **Author:** Composite, NOT one aarti: (1) Marathi verse attributed to Sant Namdev (c. 1270–c. 1350), signature "म्हणे नामा"; (2) Sanskrit "त्वमेव माता…" (Skanda Purana tradition); (3) Sanskrit "कायेन वाचा…" = Mukundamala v. 38, traditionally ascribed to Kulasekhara Alvar. All three parts pre-modern.
- **Language:** Marathi (part 1) + Sanskrit (parts 2–3)
- **Stanzas:** 3 parts only — Namdev verse + त्वमेव माता + कायेन वाचा. Achyutam Keshavam and the Hare Krishna maha-mantra are separate pieces often sung after it in booklets; they are deliberately NOT included here.

## Variant readings found and decision

- Part 1, line 3: "प्रेमें आलिंगीन आनंदे पूजीन" (Maharashtraweb) vs "प्रेमें आलिंगन, आनंदे पूजिन" (Astroyogi) vs "प्रेमें आलिंगीन आनंद पूजन" (some lyric sites) — kept "आलिंगीन … पूजीन".
- Part 1, line 4: "भावें ओवाळीन म्हणे नामा" (Maharashtraweb, Bhaktilok) — agreed.
- Part 2: "त्वमेव बन्धुश्च सखा त्वमेव" (Maharashtraweb) vs "त्वमेव बंधुश्च सखा त्वमेव" (Bhaktilok) — kept "बन्धुश्च"; "त्वमेव विद्या द्रविणं त्वमेव" — agreed (one lyric site's "द्रविडं" is a typo, not followed).
- Part 3: "कायेन वाचा मनसेन्द्रियैर्वा" (Maharashtraweb, Bhaktilok) vs "कायेन वाचा मनसेंद्रीयेव्रा" (Astroyogi, a corrupted spelling) — kept "मनसेन्द्रियैर्वा"; "बुद्ध्यात्मना वा प्रकृतिस्वभावात्" — agreed.
- Achyutam Keshavam + Hare Krishna: present in Maharashtraweb §§9 (as parts 4–5 of the sung sequence) and in the Wikisource page — deliberately LEFT OUT of this file, per the brief: they are separate pieces, not verses of Ghalin Lotangan itself.
