# येई हो विठ्ठले — SOURCE.md

- **Title (Marathi):** येई हो विठ्ठले (माझे माउली ये)
- **Deity:** Vitthal (श्री विठ्ठल)
- **Author:** Signature "विष्णुदास नामा" in stanza 3. Two traditions: (a) Sant Namdev (c. 1270–c. 1350); (b) Vishnudas Nama, a 15th-c. poet whose work is also attributed to Namdev. Either way PD. Frequently miscredited to Janabai — that attribution is NOT supported by the text (the signature is Vishnudas Nama) and is recorded here only as a misattribution to avoid.
- **Language:** Marathi
- **Stanzas:** 1 refrain (ध्रुवपद, "येई हो विठ्ठले माझे माउली ये") + 4 numbered stanzas
- **Disclaimer:** The text file carries the disclaimer line: "ही आरती पारंपरिक असून तिचे कर्तृत्व वादग्रस्त आहे — शेवटच्या कडव्यातील 'विष्णुदास नामा' या मुद्रेवरून ती विष्णुदास नामा यांची मानली जाते, काही परंपरांत ती संत नामदेव यांची म्हणून दिली जाते, आणि ती चुकून जनाबाई यांची म्हणूनही म्हटली जाते; येथे ती सामान्यपणे गायली जाते तशी दिली आहे." English: "This aarti is traditional and its authorship is disputed — the signature 'Vishnudas Nama' points to Vishnudas Nama, some traditions credit Sant Namdev, and it is also mistakenly credited to Janabai; it is given here as commonly sung."

## Variant readings found and decision

- Stanza 4 ("असो नसो भाव आम्हां तुझिया ठायां / कृपादृष्टी पाहे माझ्या पंढरीराया"): present in ShreeMarathiLyrics, MajhiMauli, and Bhaktilok; ABSENT from Maharashtraweb (which stops at 3 stanzas) and from another compared collection. Included, because three independent sources attest it.
- Stanza 3, line 1: "विठोबाचे राज्य आम्हां नित्य दीपवाळी" (Maharashtraweb, ShreeMarathiLyrics, Bhaktilok) vs "दसरा दिपवाळी" (some sung versions, e.g. Sonichits) — kept "नित्य दीपवाळी".
- Stanza 1, line 2: "पंढरपुरी आहे माझा मायबाप" — all compared sources agree.
- Stanza 2: "गरुडावरी बैसोनि माझा कैवारी आला" — all compared sources agree ("बैसून" in ShreeMarathiLyrics is the same word in modern spelling).
