# युगे अठ्ठावीस विटेवरी उभा — SOURCE.md

- **Title (Marathi):** युगे अठ्ठावीस विटेवरी उभा
- **Deity:** Vitthal / Pandurang (श्री विठ्ठल)
- **Author:** Traditionally attributed to Sant Namdev (c. 1270–c. 1350). Signature "केशवासी नामदेव भावे ओवाळिती" in the last stanza. Scholarship notes many Namdev-attributed verses are by later poets, including Vishnudas Nama (15th c.) — still PD; say "traditionally attributed to Namdev".
- **Language:** Marathi
- **Stanzas:** 5 numbered stanzas + 1 refrain (ध्रुवपद, "जय देव जय देव जय पांडुरंगा")

## Sources compared

1. Maharashtraweb — Ganpati Aarti in Marathi, §8 (Yuge Atthavis, Sant Namdev): https://www.maharashtraweb.com/prayers/ganpati-aarti
2. OmkarMyog — Shri Pandurangachi Aarti (Yuge Atthavis): https://www.omkarmyog.in/2021/03/blog-post_15.html (also checked against Shubhdarshika, https://www.shubhdarshika.in/mr/info/vitthal-aarti-yuge-atthavis, which agrees on the 5-stanza text)

## Variant readings found and decision

- A shorter 3-stanza recension of this aarti circulates in some collections, with refrain "आरती ओवाळू रखुमाईच्या वल्लभा", stanza 2 "देव मुनी जन ध्याती गरुडपारापाशीं / दर्शनमात्रे संकटे पळती भक्तांची", and stanza 3 ending "केशवचरणी नामे गाइली आरती". The 5-stanza text in our file is the one attested by Maharashtraweb, OmkarMyog, and Shubhdarshika; that 3-stanza recension was NOT followed and is recorded here.
- Stanza 3, line 1: "धन्य वेणुनाद अनुक्षेत्रपाळा" (OmkarMyog, Shubhdarshika) vs "अणुक्षेत्रपाळा" (Maharashtraweb) — kept "अनुक्षेत्रपाळा".
- Stanza 4, line 1: "कुरवंड्या येती" (Maharashtraweb) vs "कुर्वंड्या येती" (OmkarMyog) — kept "कुरवंड्या".
- Stanza 4, line 2 / stanza 5, line 2: "चंद्रभागेमाजी" (OmkarMyog) vs "चंद्रभागेमध्ये" (Maharashtraweb, stanza 5) — kept "माजी" in stanza 4 and "मध्ये" in stanza 5, as in Maharashtraweb.
- Extra stanza "धन्य पुष्पवती …" found in some booklets: NOT included — it is not attested by either compared source (Maharashtraweb, OmkarMyog) nor by Shubhdarshika. Left out deliberately; see inventory §1.
