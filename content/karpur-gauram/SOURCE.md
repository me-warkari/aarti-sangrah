# SOURCE — कर्पूरगौरम् (Karpur Gauram Karunavataram)

- Title: कर्पूरगौरं करुणावतारं (शिवयजुर्मन्त्र म्हणूनही ओळखला जातो)
- Deity: Shiva (Bhava, with Bhavani / Parvati)
- Language: Sanskrit
- Author: ancient / unattributed — no author's name has travelled with this verse. PD (ancient).
- Size: 1 shloka (4 quarters, Upajati metre).

## Variant readings found and decision taken

- No wording variants were found: all three sources print the verse identically (“कर्पूरगौरं करुणावतारं संसारसारं भुजगेन्द्रहारम् । सदा वसन्तं हृदयारविन्दे भवं भवानीसहितं नमामि ॥”). The only differences are in line-breaking and in whether “सदा वसन्तं” is written joined (“सदावसन्तं”) — spelling only.
- A common misprint in some copies reads “सदा बसन्तं हृदयारबिन्दे” / “भबं” (with ब for व/भ); it is a misprint and was not followed.

## Disclaimer — the Yajurveda question

Two traditions exist about this verse and neither is asserted here as settled. (a) Many printed booklets and recordings label it a Yajurveda mantra / Shiva Yajur Mantra. (b) Astrobhava and other critical notes point out that its Upajati metre belongs to classical Sanskrit poetry rather than to Vedic metres, and that it does not appear in the mantra index of the Shukla Yajurveda. The verse is therefore given here as an ancient Sanskrit shloka to Shiva, as commonly recited after aarti, without claiming a Vedic source.
