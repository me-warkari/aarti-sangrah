# SOURCE — गणपति अथर्वशीर्ष (Ganapati Atharvashirsha / Ganapati Upanishad)

- Title: श्री गणपत्यथर्वशीर्षम्
- Deity: Ganesha
- Language: Sanskrit (ancient)
- Author: ancient, no human author; traditionally ascribed to the Atharvaveda (a minor Upanishad; a late-medieval composition in the Atharvaveda tradition, per the University of Pennsylvania manuscript catalogue). PD (ancient).
- Size: full text — opening Shanti Path + 14 numbered sections + closing Shanti Mantra. Verified against two sources; nothing was left out as unverifiable.

## Sources compared

2. Amitray, “Ganapati Atharvashirsha: Meanings, Benefits, Lyrics and Significances” — https://amitray.com/stotram/ganapati-atharvashirsha/ — full Devanagari text, 14 verses + shanti, agreeing with Wikisource throughout. Cross-checked against Scriptle's “Gaṇapati Atharvaśīrṣa” (https://www.scriptle.org/t/ganapati-atharvashirsha/0), which records the opening-frame readings of the Nirnaya Sagar Press / Adyar prints and the Panchadeva-atharvashirsha-sangraha, and against the University of Pennsylvania manuscript record (Ms. Coll. 390, Item 100, Ganapatyatharvasirsha — http://openn.library.upenn.edu/Data/0002/html/mscoll390_item100.html).

No text, meaning, transliteration, or presentation was copied from any lyric site; the text above was written from the compared wordings.

## Variant readings found and decision taken

- Opening Shanti: Wikisource inserts, after “स्वस्ति नो बृहस्पतिर्दधातु”, the four lines “ॐ तन्मामवतु तद् वक्तारमवतु अवतु माम् अवतु वक्तारम्”. Scriptle's source audit (2026-10-18) records that those four lines are the tail of the Taittiriya “शं नो मित्रः” shanti (Taittiriya Upanishad 1.1.1), not part of the “भद्रं कर्णेभिः” shanti, and that no print consulted (Nirnaya Sagar Press, Adyar, Panchadeva-sangraha) has them here; Amitray's text also omits them. Decision: **omit** the तन्मामवतु lines from the opening; the opening here is भद्रं कर्णेभिः + स्वस्ति न इन्द्रः + ॐ शान्तिः, as in the prints. The variant is recorded here so a reader whose booklet includes them is not surprised.
- Section 6: Wikisource reads “त्वं मूलाधारः स्थिथोऽसि नित्यम्” — “स्थिथो” is a typographical error for “स्थितो”. Decision: corrected to “स्थितोऽसि” (as in Amitray and all prints).
- Section 2: Wikisource gives “ऋतं वच्मि (वदिष्यामि) ॥ सत्यं वच्मि (वदिष्यामि) ॥” — the parenthetical is Wikisource's gloss of the alternative reading. Decision: kept as in Wikisource, parentheses included, so the alternative is visible.
- Section 8/9 numbering: Wikisource numbers the Gayatri ॥८॥ and the Dhyana (Ekadantam) ॥९॥ separately; some booklets merge them into one section. Decision: keep Wikisource's separate numbering (14 sections total).
- Closing Shanti: Wikisource closes with “ॐ सहनाववतु …” followed by the full भद्रं कर्णेभिः shanti again. Some recensions close with the भद्रं कर्णेभिः shanti only, without सहनाववतु. Decision: keep Wikisource's full closing; record the shorter closing here as a variant.
- A mangala shloka “यं नत्वा मुनयः …” appears before the shanti in some prints (NSP, Adyar); Scriptle notes it is not part of the chant recension. Decision: not included.
