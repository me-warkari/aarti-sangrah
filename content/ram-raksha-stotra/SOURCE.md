# SOURCE — श्री रामरक्षा स्तोत्र (Ram Raksha Stotra)

- Title: श्री रामरक्षा स्तोत्र
- Deity: Rama
- Language: Sanskrit
- Author: Budha Kaushika (बुधकौशिकऋषिः — named in the viniyoga and in the closing colophon "इति श्रीबुधकौशिकविरचितं"). Traditional/ancient; the stotra is traditionally said to have been revealed to Budha Kaushika in a dream by Shiva (verse 15). PD (ancient).
- Size: viniyoga (5 lines) + dhyana (4 lines) + 38 numbered verses + closing colophon. Complete as given on Wikisource.

## Variant readings / Wikisource spellings kept verbatim

The sa.wikisource page has several spellings that look like typing quirks; all are kept exactly as on Wikisource pending proofing against a printed edition — do not "correct" them silently:

- Viniyoga: "श्रीरामरक्षास्तोञ मंञस्य" (स्तोञ for स्तोत्र, मंञस्य for मन्त्रस्य).
- Verse 13: "सिध्दयः" (for सिद्धयः). Verse 14: "वज्रपज्जरनामेदं" (for वज्रपञ्जर-). Verse 15: "प्रबुध्दो". Verse 24: "श्रध्दयान्वितः". Verse 33: "बुध्दिमतां". Verse 37: "मामुध्दर". Verse 38: "सहस्त्रनामतत्त्युलं" (for तत्तुल्यं).
- Verse 12: "र्भुक्तिं" (for भुक्तिं). Verse 25: "नामभिर्दिवैर्न" — kept as printed.
