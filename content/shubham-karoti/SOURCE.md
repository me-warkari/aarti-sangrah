# SOURCE — शुभं करोति (Shubham Karoti — lamp prayer)

- Title: शुभं करोति कल्याणम् (दीप मंत्र)
- Deity: the lamp flame (Deepa Jyoti), as Parabrahman / Janardana (Vishnu); sung at evening lamp-lighting (sandhya)
- Language: Sanskrit (traditional)
- Author: traditional / unattributed (smriti verses handed down in household sandhya practice; sometimes traced to Skanda Purana / Deepa Jyoti traditions — see Disclaimer). PD (ancient/traditional).
- Size: 2 shlokas, recited together.

## Sources compared

1. Ottawa Hindu Temple library, “Shubham Karoti — Evening Lamp Prayer” — https://ottawahindutemple.com/library/mantra/shubham-karoti-deep-mantra — full Devanagari text, 2 shlokas, described as “traditional smriti verses — sandhya deep-darshan”. Used as the base wording.
2. Mahakatha, “Shubham Karoti Kalyanam: Lamp Prayer for Prosperity” — https://mahakatha.com/mantras/universal/shubham-karoti-kalyanam/ — full Devanagari text, the same 2 shlokas. Agrees word for word. Cross-checked against BhaktiVerse's daily-shlokas page (https://www.bhaktiverse.com/mantra/daily-shlokas/), which prints the same two shlokas.

No text, meaning, transliteration, or presentation was copied from any lyric site; the text above was written from the compared wordings.

## Variant readings found and decision taken

- Verse 1: “धनसंपदा” (Ottawa, Mahakatha) vs “धनसंपदः” (some prints, e.g. Mantra Deepika) — same word, sandhi spelling only; kept “धनसंपदा”.
- “दीपज्योतिर्नमोऽस्तु ते” (Ottawa, Mahakatha) vs “दीपज्योतिर्नमोऽस्तुते” (many prints): kept “नमोऽस्तु ते”.
- Verse 2: “दीपज्योतिः परब्रह्म” (Ottawa, Mahakatha) vs “दीपज्योतिः परं ब्रह्म” (an extended Deepa Jyoti Stotram in some collections): kept “परब्रह्म”.
- Verse 2, last line: “दीपज्योतिर्नमोऽस्तु ते” (Ottawa, Mahakatha, BhaktiVerse) vs “संध्यादीप नमोऽस्तुते” in some traditions (an extended version in some collections): kept “दीपज्योतिर्नमोऽस्तु ते”; the संध्यादीप ending is a regional variant, recorded here.
- Longer Deepa Jyoti Stotram compilations add further verses (दीपं ज्योति परं ब्रह्म …, त्वं ज्योतिषां परं ज्योतिः …, तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय) after these two. Decision: include only the two shlokas that are universally recited together as “Shubham Karoti”; the further verses belong to the larger Deepa Jyoti Stotram and are not part of this piece.

## Disclaimer

These are traditional household verses; no author is named in any source checked, and the Skanda Purana / Brihadaranyaka origin sometimes claimed for them is not established by the sources compared — it is recorded here as a claim, not as a fact.
