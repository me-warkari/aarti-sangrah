स्रोत — ॐ जय जगदीश हरे

विष्णू · पं. श्रद्धा राम फिल्लौरी · हिंदी · परवाना: सार्वजनिक क्षेत्र (Public Domain) · तपासणी बाकी

← ॐ जय जगदीश हरे वाचा

  • Title: ॐ जय जगदीश हरे / श्री जगदीशजी की आरती
  • Deity: Vishnu (Jagdish, Lord of the Universe)
  • Language: Hindi (not Marathi — labelled Hindi on the page)
  • Author: Pandit Shraddha Ram Phillauri (1837–1881, d. 24 June 1881); composed c. 1870. PD in India (life + 60) and the US (pre-1930).
  • Size: refrain + 8 verses + closing couplet.

Variant readings found and decision taken

  • Closing couplet, second line: एका स्रोतात reads “कहत शिवानन्द स्वामी, सुख सम्पत्ति पावे”; एका स्रोतात and another compared source read “कहत शिवानंद स्वामी, मनवाँछित फल पावे”. Decision: follow एका स्रोतात (“सुख सम्पत्ति पावे”); record the other reading here. The couplet itself is a separate tradition — it names “शिवानन्द स्वामी”, not Phillauri — so it is kept as a closing couplet, not attributed to Phillauri.
  • Refrain: some versions (एका स्रोतात stanza 1) omit the repeated “दास जनों के संकट” line; एका स्रोतात and एका स्रोतात include it. Decision: include it, as in the most widely sung form.
  • “सन्तन की सेवा” (एका स्रोतात) vs “संतन की सेवा” (most other versions): spelling only; kept एका स्रोतात's “सन्तन”.
  • Verse count: 8 verses + closing is consistent across both sources; some pages count the refrain as a stanza, giving “9 stanzas” — that is a counting difference, not a text difference.

Disclaimer

This text is traditional and given as commonly sung. The closing “कहत शिवानन्द स्वामी” couplet comes from a separate tradition and is not part of Phillauri's original composition as far as the sources compared establish; it is included because it is sung with the aarti in most homes.
