  • Work: Dhoop Aarti (धूप आरती), evening aarti of Shirdi Sai Baba, sung at sunset
  • Deity: Shirdi Sai Baba (Sadguru Sainath)
  • Status: INCLUDED — PARTIAL. Only the parts below are included. The Namaskarashtak (Mohani Raj) sung in some lists is left out (see "Left out" below) because its author cannot be verified.
  • Licence for this text: PD-marked originals (see per-part verdicts). No modern translation, commentary, recording, or booklet layout is included.
  • Proofing: NEEDED. Text written from the wordings compared below; it has not had independent Marathi proofreading. Report errors via the site's "Report an error" link.
  • Disclaimer: attributions are traditional, taken from the parts' own signatures and from the Sagunopasana tradition; where two traditions exist both are recorded. We are not fighting for authority — the text is given as commonly sung.

Left out — and why

  • Namaskarashtak — अनंता तुला तें कसें रे स्तवावें, credited to "Shri Mohani Raj" (signature "भवीं मोहनीराजा" in the last verse). No biographical trace of Mohani Raj was found in any source checked — no birth or death year, no life, no first-publication date. The piece is one of the 16 Sai-specific psalms in the Sagunopasana account, which shows it existed by the time of the booklet's formation (c. 1908–1923), but the edition in which it first appeared could not be established, so neither life+60 nor pre-1930 publication can be verified. Left out as unverifiable. It can be revisited if a death year ≤1965, or a dated pre-1930 Sagunopasana edition containing it, is located. In the Dhoop order it stands between the Naman and Aisa Yei Ba; that position is noted here so a singer can see where it belongs.

Variant readings and ordering noted

  • Part order: A orders the Dhoop as Adkar, Dasganu, Naman (+ Nama Smaran), Namaskarashtak, Prarthana, Mahimna, Guru Prasada, एका स्रोतात Pushpam. B and the other English lists give the same order. This file follows A, minus the excluded Namaskarashtak.
  • Part 1 (Adkar): A prints the refrain again at the end of the aarti; printed once at the head here.
  • Part 3 (Naman): A separates the Hare Rama / Hare Krishna एका स्रोतात as its own numbered part ("नाम स्मरणं"); here it is v. 5 of the Naman, as in the Udupi listing, with "श्री गुरुदेव दत्त" as its closing line.
  • Part 4 (Aisa Yei Ba): "बाबा येई बा" (C, B) vs "(बाबाये) महाराजे ईबा" (A). The "बाबा येई बा" reading is used; the variant is recorded here.
  • Part 5 (Mahimna): A ends the Mahimna at "स्तोत्रमेतत्पठेद्भक्त्या" (v. 15 here) and prints the Karacharana verse after the एका स्रोतात Pushpam; the Madhyan file, following Udupi, prints Karacharana at the end of the Mahimna instead. The same verses therefore appear in different positions in the two files — a genuine ordering variant, not a duplication error.
  • Part 7 (एका स्रोतात Pushpam): the Karacharana verse ("करचरणकृतं…") and "जय जय करुणाब्धे" are printed here as vv. 5–6 of the closing, as in A.
  • Part 6, v. 9: A prints the "विमूढ म्हणुनि हसो … प्रपंचि मनहे रुसो" block as one continuous verse; kept as one numbered verse here.

This is a claim over the website's own content, not a claim over the underlying traditional aarti texts.
  • The Sansthan states the copyright of the Shri Sai Satcharita book belongs to it and requires permission to reproduce it. That claim covers the Satcharita (a different work, not included here), not these aarti texts.
  • The Sansthan publishes a revised edition of Bhishma's Sagunopasana since 1923 and calls it the official Book of Daily Worship; that is a claim over its own edition/compilation and over its recordings and live-darshan broadcasts. No live Sansthan claim was found covering the underlying PD texts themselves.
  • Conclusion applied here: the underlying texts by authors dead ≤1965 are republished from PD sources as above; no Sansthan booklet text, layout, translation, or recording is copied. If the Sansthan asserts a claim over a specific text here, that part will be removed and the claim recorded.
