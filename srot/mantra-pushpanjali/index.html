स्रोत — मंत्रपुष्पांजली

समापन · वैदिक (प्राचीन) · संस्कृत · परवाना: सार्वजनिक क्षेत्र (Public Domain) · तपासणी बाकी

← मंत्रपुष्पांजली वाचा

  • Title: मंत्रपुष्पांजली (मन्त्रपुष्पाञ्जलि)
  • Deity: none single — closing Vedic prayer offered with flowers at the end of aartis, to the deity of the puja
  • Language: Sanskrit (ancient, Vedic)
  • Author: ancient, no human author. The five hymns are drawn from Vedic sources: hymn 1 from Rigveda 1.164.50 (also 10.90.16; Atharvaveda 7.5.1); hymn 2 from Taittiriya Aranyaka 1.31.6; hymns 3–4 from Aitareya Brahmana VIII.15 and VIII.21; hymn 5 from Rigveda 10.81.3. It is an appendix of the Shukla Yajurveda recital called Deve. PD (ancient).
  • Size: 5 hymns.

Variant readings found and decision taken

  • Svara marks: Wएका स्रोतात prints the Vedic accents (य॒ज्ञेन॑ etc.); एका स्रोतात and the common recited form print the text unaccented. Decision: unaccented plain Devanagari, as recited in aarti practice; the accents are a chanting aid, not part of the words.
  • Hymn 3, last word: “एकराळिति” (Wएका स्रोतात) vs “एकराट् इति” in some prints. Decision: “एकराळिति”, following Wएका स्रोतात's text.
  • Hymn 4: “आविक्षितस्य कामप्रेर्विश्वे देवाः सभासद इति” — Wएका स्रोतात and एका स्रोतात agree; some copies read “कामप्रेः”. Decision: “कामप्रेर्”.
  • Hymn 5 (“विश्वतश्चक्षुः”): Wएका स्रोतात notes it is “often skipped in currently prevalent recitals of एका स्रोतात Pushpanjali performed after Arati”, but that the Deve scriptures include it. Decision: include it, numbered ॥५॥, and note the practice here so a reader who skips it is not surprised.
  • Opening “ॐ” and “ॐ स्वस्ति” before hymns 1 and 3 are later additions to the original Vedic text (per Wएका स्रोतात); they are kept because they are part of the recited form.
  • The common recited form ends with “मंत्रपुष्पांजली समर्पयामि” / “श्रीशुभं भवतु” in some booklets; those are closing utterances, not part of the five hymns, and are not included as text.
