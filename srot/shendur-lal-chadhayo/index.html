स्रोत — शेंदुर लाल चढायो

गणेश · पारंपरिक (हिंदी) · हिंदी · परवाना: सार्वजनिक क्षेत्र (Public Domain) · तपासणी बाकी

← शेंदुर लाल चढायो वाचा

  • Title: शेंदुर लाल चढायो अच्छा गजमुख को
  • Deity: एका स्रोतातa
  • Language: Hindi (not Marathi — labelled Hindi on the page; the wording mixes Hindi/Braj and Marathi forms, as sung in Maharashtra)
  • Author: traditional / unattributed. The last verse contains the signature-like word “गोसावीनंदन”; no old manuscript or scholarly edition establishes Morya Gosavi as the author, so the Morya Gosavi attribution is NOT asserted. PD as a traditional text circulated for generations.
  • Size: 3 stanzas + 1 refrain (the refrain, धृ, is repeated after each stanza when sung).

Variant readings found and decision taken

  • “दोंदिल लाल” (एका स्रोतात एका स्रोतात) vs “दोंदिलाल” in some copies: kept “दोंदिल लाल”.
  • “सुत गौरिहर को” (एका स्रोतात एका स्रोतात) vs “सुत गौरीहर को” (एका स्रोतात, एका स्रोतात): kept “गौरिहर”.
  • “सांई सुरवर को” (एका स्रोतात एका स्रोतात) vs “साईं सुरवर को” (एका स्रोतात): kept “सांई”.
  • “संतत संपत सबही भरपूर पावे” (एका स्रोतात एका स्रोतात) vs “संतति संपत्ति सभी भरपूर पावे” (एका स्रोतात): kept “संतत संपत सबही”.
  • “गुण गावे” (एका स्रोतात) vs “गुन गावे” (एका स्रोतात): kept “गुण गावे”.
  • “कोटी सूरजप्रकाश” (एका स्रोतात) vs “कोटि सूरज प्रकाश” (एका स्रोतात): kept एका स्रोतात's form.
  • “शशिबिहारी” (एका स्रोतात) vs “शशिबिहारि” (एका स्रोतात): kept “शशिबिहारी”.
  • Refrain: “जय जय श्री गणराज” (एका स्रोतात एका स्रोतात) vs “जय जय जी गणराज” (एका स्रोतात, many recordings): kept “श्री गणराज”; the “जी” form is a sung variant. Some versions insert “जय देव जय देव” before the refrain; it is a sung interjection, not a verse, and is not included as text.
  • One site (Hindukosh) gives a different second stanza (“अष्टौ सिद्धि नवनिधि दातार … पारवती नंदन शिवसुत त्राता”), which belongs to a different एका स्रोतात aarti; it was excluded as a conflation, not a variant of this text.

Disclaimer

This text is traditional and given as commonly sung. Its authorship is disputed/unestablished: the word “गोसावीनंदन” in the last verse looks like a poet's signature, but no source checked names an author on the strength of an old manuscript or scholarly edition, so no author is asserted here. The 1999 film version is a separate, copyrighted arrangement and is not the source of this text.
