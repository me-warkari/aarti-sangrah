  • Work: Shej Aarti (शेज आरती), night aarti of Shirdi Sai Baba, sung c. 10:00–10:30 PM
  • Deity: Shirdi Sai Baba (Sadguru Sainath)
  • Status: INCLUDED — COMPLETE as per the standard lists checked. All seven parts below verify as public domain; nothing was left out.
  • Licence for this text: PD-marked originals (see per-part verdicts). No modern translation, commentary, recording, or booklet layout is included.
  • Proofing: NEEDED. Text written from the wordings compared below; it has not had independent Marathi proofreading. Report errors via the site's "Report an error" link.
  • Disclaimer: attributions are traditional, taken from the parts' own signatures and from the Sagunopasana tradition. The second part (Aarti Dnyanraja) is disputed: the Shej lists credit it to Rama Janardhani Swami, while other collections credit the same aarti to Samarth Ramdas or to Sant Namdev. All three traditions are recorded below; we do not settle the dispute and we are not fighting for authority — the text is given as commonly sung.

Author-date evidence (checked live 2026-11-17/18):

  • Tukaram (1608–1649/50) — standard Varkari dates; Rameshwar Bhat of Wagholi, 17th c., the Brahmin who demanded Tukaram sink his notebooks in the Indrayani and later became his disciple and composed abhangs in his praise — एका स्रोतात, "Tukaram Sources" (एका स्रोतात), citing the Gatha tradition and Nana Maharaj Sakhare's 1990 Gatha printing his abhangs; also एका स्रोतात, Shej Aarti post ("composed by Rameshwar Bhat, his disciple").
  • Rama Janardhani Swami — no independent life/dates found in the sources checked. The credit rests on the signature "रामजनार्दनीं" in the last verse and on the Sagunopasana account ("How K. J. Bhishma Composed Shri Sadguru Sainath Sagunopasana", V. B. Kher, from Shri Sai Leela, Sep 1985), which counts "one each by Shri. Ramjanardhan Swami and Shri. Rameshwar Bhat" among the 14 traditional psalms of the Book of Shirdi Aratis. The same aarti is credited to Ramdas in some collections and to Namdev in others (see the aarti-sangrah inventory, §3: Aarti Dnyanraja — attribution disputed, Ramdas vs Namdev). Since every candidate is pre-modern, the PD verdict does not depend on settling the dispute; all three traditions are recorded here and in the file's header note.
  • Bhishma b. 16 Aug 1854, d. 8 Aug 1935 — एका स्रोतात, "Krishnashastri Jageshwar Bhishma"; Sagunopasana history as in the Kakad SOURCE.md.

Left out

Nothing. All seven parts in the standard Shej lists verify as PD and are included.

Variant readings noted

  • Part 1: A numbers the stanzas 1, 2, 4, 4 (an evident numbering slip); corrected here to 1–4. The stanza texts agree across A, B, and D.
  • Part 2: the refrain "आरती ज्ञानराजा" is printed at the head here; A and B repeat it after every stanza. The last verse reads "रामजनार्दनीं पायीं मस्तक ठेविलें" in A and B; some Marathi lists print "राम जनार्दनी". Same reading.
  • Part 3, v. 1: A prints "(जैसे) पाषाण तारीलें" and "(हे) तुकोबाचे … (उदकी) रक्षिलें" with bracketed words; the brackets are editorial insertions in A and are dropped here, following B and C ("जैसे पाषाण तारीलें / तैसे हे तुकोबाचे अभंग रक्षिलें").
  • Part 4, v. 3: "सोडुनि जाया दुःख वाटतें बाबांचा चरणांसी हो" (A) vs "साईंचा चरणांसी हो" in some wordings (C gives "Baba tava charanaansee" in one line and "Sayee tava charanaansee" in the repeat). The A reading is used.
  • Part 5, v. 6: A notes the variant "निजविल" for "निजे शेजेला"; the A main reading "निरंजन सद्गुरु स्वामी निजे शेजेला" is used.
  • Part 6, v. 2: "झालों एकसवा" (A) — some lists print "झालो आता एकसवा"; the A reading is used.
  • Part 7: the refrain "आतां स्वामी सुखें निद्रा करा गोपाळा" repeats after every stanza in A and B; printed within each numbered verse here, as in A.
