साईबाबा · अनेक संत — तुकाराम, जनाबाई, नामदेव, भिष्म, दासगणू · मराठी + हिंदी · परवाना: सार्वजनिक क्षेत्र (Public Domain) · तपासणी बाकी

← काकड आरती (साईबाबा) वाचा

  • Work: Kakad Aarti (काकड आरती), morning aarti of Shirdi Sai Baba, sung c. 5:15 AM
  • Deity: Shirdi Sai Baba (Sadguru Sainath)
  • Status: INCLUDED — PARTIAL. Only the parts below are included. Two parts sung in some lists are left out (see "Left out" below) because their authorship / death year could not be verified. This file is therefore a partial Kakad Aarti, not the full temple list.
  • Licence for this text: PD-marked originals (see per-part verdicts). No modern translation, commentary, recording, or booklet layout is included.
  • Proofing: NEEDED. Text written from the two Devanagari wordings compared below; it has not had independent Marathi proofreading. Report errors via the site's "Report an error" link.
  • Disclaimer: attributions are traditional, taken from the parts' own signatures (mudra) and from the Sagunopasana tradition; where two traditions exist both are recorded. We are not fighting for authority — the text is given as commonly sung.

Author-date evidence (checked live 2026-11-17/18):

  • Bhishma b. 16 Aug 1854, d. 8 Aug 1935 — एका स्रोतात, "Krishnashastri Jageshwar Bhishma" page; corroborated by the Sai Satcharita (Ch. 4 / 1912 entry: "Mr. Krishnarao Jageshwar Bhisma, the author of the pamphlet Sai Sagunopasana").
  • Sagunopasana history — "How K. J. Bhishma Composed Shri Sadguru Sainath Sagunopasana" (V. B. Kher, from Shri Sai Leela, Sep 1985), hosted by Shirdi Sai Trust: Bhishma composed the booklet verse by verse before Baba (1911–12); Khaparde published it at his expense till 1922; after the Sansthan was founded in 1923 a revised edition was published by the Sansthan with Bhishma's consent, with more compositions added. The same account counts the 30 psalms of the Book of Shirdi Aratis: 16 especially on Sai Baba (9 Bhishma, 3 Dasganu, 1 each Upasani, Adkar, Mohani Raj, B. V. Deo) and 14 traditional (5 Tukaram, 2 each Namdev and Janabai, 1 each Ramjanardhan Swami and Rameshwar Bhat, 1 Vedic hymn, 2 traditional prayers).
  • Dasganu b. 6 Jan 1868, d. 26 Nov 1962 — एका स्रोतात, "Das Ganu" page (samadhi 26 Nov 1962, Pandharpur); the 1963 date appears in some blogs and in a scholarly chapter (Deák, citing Dasganu d. 1963). Both dates are ≤1965, so the PD verdict does not turn on which is right; both are recorded here.
  • Tukaram (1608–1649/50), Namdev (c. 1270–c. 1350), Janabai (13th–14th c.) — standard Varkari dates; PD by centuries.

Left out — and why

  • Bhajan "साईनाथ गुरु माझे आई" (listed between parts 8 and 9 in some booklets), attributed to Smt. Savitribai Raghunath Tendulkar, author of Shri Sainath Bhajan Mala. No death year for Savitribai Tendulkar was found in any source checked (she was a contemporary of Baba, active in the 1910s; her great-grandson describes the book as her legacy, with no dates). Authorship is established but PD status is unverifiable, so the bhajan is left out. It can be revisited if a death year ≤1965 (or a pre-1930 printed edition) is found.
  • "श्रीसद्गुरु पद" — श्रीसद्गुरु बाबा साई, तुजवांचुनि आश्रय नाहीं भूतली (listed as a closing pad in some Kakad lists). No source checked names an author for it, and no death year or first publication could be established. Left out as unverifiable; revisit if an author is established.

Variant readings noted

  • Part 1, v. 1: "परिसावी विनंती माझी सद्गुरुनाथा" (B and most sources) vs "पंढरीनाथा" (A). The majority reading is used; the variant is recorded here.
  • Part 3: some sources print the refrain only once; A prints it after every stanza. The refrain is printed once at the head of the part here, to be repeated in singing.
  • Part 4: the opening is given as "उठा पांडुरंगा आतां दर्शन द्या सकळां" (A) in some lists and as a continuation of part 2 in others; treated here as its own part, as in A and in the English aarti lists.
  • Part 5, v. 2: "ओंवाळूं हा रमाधवा" (A) vs "रामाधवा / रघुमाधवा" in other wordings. The A reading is used.
  • Part 7: some lists give 3 stanzas; A gives 4 (with "राई रखुमाबाई" as v. 3). The 4-stanza form is used.
  • Part 10 vs 11: the two Hindi Dasganu pads are distinct texts ("साई रहम नजर करना" and "रहम नजर करो"); some lists print only one. Both are included, as in A.
  • Part 11, v. 2–4: A prints the refrain "रहम नजर करो" after each stanza; the stanza text is as given. Verse numbering here follows the stanzas, not the refrain repeats.
  • Part 12: given as one continuous pad in A, without stanza numbers; split here into two numbered verses at the natural break (the repeat of the opening line).

Sansthan position (checked live 2026-11-17/18)

  • Shri Saibaba Sansthan Trust, Shirdi, official site (एका स्रोतात.in, Copyright page): "This web site is Copyright by the Shri Saibaba Sansthan Trust (Shirdi). All Rights including Copy Rights Reserved… No artwork, images or articles may be copied to another web site without prior written permission." This is a claim over the website's own content, not a claim over the underlying traditional aarti texts.
  • The Sansthan states the copyright of the Shri Sai Satcharita book belongs to it and requires permission to reproduce it. That claim covers the Satcharita (a different work, not included here), not these aarti texts.
  • The Sansthan publishes a revised edition of Bhishma's Sagunopasana since 1923 and calls it the official Book of Daily Worship; that is a claim over its own edition/compilation and over its recordings and live-darshan broadcasts ("Shirdi live darshan is the copyright of Shirdi Sai Sansthan"). No live Sansthan claim was found covering the underlying PD texts themselves.
  • Conclusion applied here: the underlying texts by authors dead ≤1965 are republished from PD sources as above; no Sansthan booklet text, layout, translation, or recording is copied, and no Sansthan material is reproduced. If the Sansthan asserts a claim over a specific text here, that part will be removed and the claim recorded.
