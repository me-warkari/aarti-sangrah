  • Title: गवळण — गाऱ्हाणें सांगाया, आल्या गोकुळीच्या स्त्रिया
  • Deity: Krishna (child Krishna of Gokul; the gopis complain to Yashoda)
  • Language: Marathi
  • Author: traditionally attributed to Sant Namdev (c. 1270–c. 1350; the Gatha text signs “नामा म्हणे”). PD either way — see Disclaimer. The attribution is traditional: scholarship notes many Namdev-attributed verses are by later poets, but all are pre-modern and PD.
  • Size: 11 couplets.

Scope — read this first

“Gavlani” is a genre, not a single song: milkmaid songs in which the gopis of Gokul sing of Krishna — going to Mathura market, his flute, his pranks, their complaints (गाऱ्हाणें) to Yashoda. There is no one canonical “Gavlani”. This file therefore gives one complete, verifiable, traditionally-attributed gavlani — the गाऱ्हाणें from the Namdev Abhang Gatha — and does not pretend it is the only gavlani.

Variant readings found and decision taken

  • None recorded: with a single typed source there is nothing to compare against. The Wएका स्रोतात Gatha text is reproduced as found, including its old spellings. Any modernised spelling in sung versions (e.g. “गाऱ्हाणी” sung as “गार्‍हाणी”) is a performance variant, not recorded as a textual variant.

Disclaimer

“Gavlani” names a genre of Marathi Krishna songs, not one fixed composition — this file gives one traditional piece from the Namdev tradition, as commonly sung, and does not claim it is the only or the authoritative gavlani. Its attribution to Namdev is traditional; the Gatha itself signs it “नामा म्हणे”.
