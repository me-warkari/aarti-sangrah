  • Title: श्री गणपत्यथर्वशीर्षम्
  • Deity: एका स्रोतातa
  • Language: Sanskrit (ancient)
  • Author: ancient, no human author; traditionally ascribed to the Atharvaveda (a minor Upanishad; a late-medieval composition in the Atharvaveda tradition, per the University of Pennsylvania manuscript catalogue). PD (ancient).
  • Size: full text — opening Shanti Path + 14 numbered sections + closing Shanti एका स्रोतात. Verified against two sources; nothing was left out as unverifiable.

Variant readings found and decision taken

  • Opening Shanti: Wएका स्रोतात inserts, after “स्वस्ति नो बृहस्पतिर्दधातु”, the four lines “ॐ तन्मामवतु तद् वक्तारमवतु अवतु माम् अवतु वक्तारम्”. एका स्रोतात's source audit (2026-10-18) records that those four lines are the tail of the Taittiriya “शं नो मित्रः” shanti (Taittiriya Upanishad 1.1.1), not part of the “भद्रं कर्णेभिः” shanti, and that no print consulted (Nirnaya Sagar Press, Adyar, Panchadeva-sangraha) has them here; एका स्रोतात's text also omits them. Decision: omit the तन्मामवतु lines from the opening; the opening here is भद्रं कर्णेभिः + स्वस्ति न इन्द्रः + ॐ शान्तिः, as in the prints. The variant is recorded here so a reader whose booklet includes them is not surprised.
  • Section 6: Wएका स्रोतात reads “त्वं मूलाधारः स्थिथोऽसि नित्यम्” — “स्थिथो” is a typographical error for “स्थितो”. Decision: corrected to “स्थितोऽसि” (as in एका स्रोतात and all prints).
  • Section 2: Wएका स्रोतात gives “ऋतं वच्मि (वदिष्यामि) ॥ सत्यं वच्मि (वदिष्यामि) ॥” — the parenthetical is Wएका स्रोतात's gloss of the alternative reading. Decision: kept as in Wएका स्रोतात, parentheses included, so the alternative is visible.
  • Section 8/9 numbering: Wएका स्रोतात numbers the Gayatri ॥८॥ and the Dhyana (Ekadantam) ॥९॥ separately; some booklets merge them into one section. Decision: keep Wएका स्रोतात's separate numbering (14 sections total).
  • Closing Shanti: Wएका स्रोतात closes with “ॐ सहनाववतु …” followed by the full भद्रं कर्णेभिः shanti again. Some recensions close with the भद्रं कर्णेभिः shanti only, without सहनाववतु. Decision: keep Wएका स्रोतात's full closing; record the shorter closing here as a variant.
  • A mangala shloka “यं नत्वा मुनयः …” appears before the shanti in some prints (NSP, Adyar); एका स्रोतात notes it is not part of the chant recension. Decision: not included.
