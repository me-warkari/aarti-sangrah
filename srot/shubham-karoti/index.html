स्रोत — शुभं करोति

विष्णू · पारंपरिक · संस्कृत · परवाना: सार्वजनिक क्षेत्र (Public Domain) · तपासणी बाकी

← शुभं करोति वाचा

  • Title: शुभं करोति कल्याणम् (दीप मंत्र)
  • Deity: the lamp flame (Deepa Jyoti), as Parabrahman / Janardana (Vishnu); sung at evening lamp-lighting (sandhya)
  • Language: Sanskrit (traditional)
  • Author: traditional / unattributed (smriti verses handed down in household sandhya practice; sometimes traced to Skanda Purana / Deepa Jyoti traditions — see Disclaimer). PD (ancient/traditional).
  • Size: 2 shlokas, recited together.

Variant readings found and decision taken

  • Verse 1: “धनसंपदा” (एका स्रोतात, एका स्रोतात) vs “धनसंपदः” (some prints, e.g. एका स्रोतात Deepika) — same word, sandhi spelling only; kept “धनसंपदा”.
  • “दीपज्योतिर्नमोऽस्तु ते” (एका स्रोतात, एका स्रोतात) vs “दीपज्योतिर्नमोऽस्तुते” (many prints): kept “नमोऽस्तु ते”.
  • Verse 2: “दीपज्योतिः परब्रह्म” (एका स्रोतात, एका स्रोतात) vs “दीपज्योतिः परं ब्रह्म” (an extended Deepa Jyoti Stotram in some collections): kept “परब्रह्म”.
  • Verse 2, last line: “दीपज्योतिर्नमोऽस्तु ते” (एका स्रोतात, एका स्रोतात एका स्रोतात) vs “संध्यादीप नमोऽस्तुते” in some traditions (an extended version in some collections): kept “दीपज्योतिर्नमोऽस्तु ते”; the संध्यादीप ending is a regional variant, recorded here.
  • Longer Deepa Jyoti Stotram compilations add further verses (दीपं ज्योति परं ब्रह्म …, त्वं ज्योतिषां परं ज्योतिः …, तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय) after these two. Decision: include only the two shlokas that are universally recited together as “Shubham Karoti”; the further verses belong to the larger Deepa Jyoti Stotram and are not part of this piece.

Disclaimer

These are traditional household verses; no author is named in any source checked, and the Skanda Purana / Brihadaranyaka origin sometimes claimed for them is not established by the sources compared — it is recorded here as a claim, not as a fact.
