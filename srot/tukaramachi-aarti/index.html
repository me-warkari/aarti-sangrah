स्रोत — तुकारामाची आरती

तुकाराम · रामेश्वर भट · मराठी · परवाना: सार्वजनिक क्षेत्र (Public Domain) · तपासणी बाकी

← तुकारामाची आरती वाचा

  • Title (Marathi): तुकारामाची आरती (आरती तुकारामा)
  • Deity: Sant Tukaram (संत तुकाराम महाराज, as deity — this aarti is TO Tukaram, not BY him)
  • Author: Traditionally attributed to Rameshwar Bhat, a disciple of Tukaram (17th c.), per the Shirdi Sai Shej Aarti account ("Aarti of Saint Tukaram Maharaj, composed by Rameshwar Bhat, his disciple"); the last stanza signs "रामेश्वरे". Other compared sources list the aarti simply as traditional, without naming an author. Both are recorded; the Rameshwar Bhat attribution rests on that one account.
  • Language: Marathi
  • Stanzas: 1 refrain (ध्रुवपद, "आरती तुकारामा") + 2 numbered stanzas
  • Disclaimer: The text file carries the disclaimer line: "ही आरती तुकाराम महाराजांवर आहे, त्यांनी रचलेली नाही — ती त्यांचे शिष्य रामेश्वर भट यांची मानली जाते; काही स्रोत मात्र तिला केवळ 'पारंपरिक' म्हणून नोंदवतात, कर्त्याचे नाव देत नाहीत. येथे ती सामान्यपणे गायली जाते तशी दिली आहे." English: "This aarti is about Tukaram Maharaj, not composed by him — it is attributed to his disciple Rameshwar Bhat, while some sources list it only as 'traditional' without naming an author; it is given here as commonly sung."

Variant readings found and decision

  • Stanza 1, line 1: "राघवे सागरात । पाषाण तारियेले" (एका स्रोतात) vs "पाषाण तारिले" (Shirdi Sai account, एका स्रोतात) — kept "तारियेले" (एका स्रोतात); "तारिले" recorded as the variant.
  • Stanza 1, line 2: "अभंग उदकी रक्षिले" (एका स्रोतात, एका स्रोतात) vs "अभंग उदकीं रक्षिले" (Shirdi Sai account) — kept "उदकी"; spelling only.
  • Refrain: "आरती तुकारामा । स्वामी सद्गुरुधामा । सच्चिदानंदमूर्ती । पाय दाखवी आम्हां" — all compared sources agree. One source adds "जय देव जय देव" after the refrain — a sung expansion, not included.
  • A different, longer Tukaram aarti circulates in some collections ("प्रपंचरचना सर्वहि भोगूनि त्यागिली …", refrain "जय जयाजी सद्गुरु तुकया दातारा") — that is a different composition and was NOT used here.
