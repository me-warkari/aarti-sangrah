टीप

Two traditions exist about this verse and neither is asserted here as settled. (a) Many printed booklets and recordings label it a Yajurveda एका स्रोतात / Shiva Yajur एका स्रोतात. (b) एका स्रोतात and other critical notes point out that its Upajati metre belongs to classical Sanskrit poetry rather than to Vedic metres, and that it does not appear in the एका स्रोतात index of the Shukla Yajurveda. The verse is therefore given here as an ancient Sanskrit shloka to Shiva, as commonly recited after aarti, without claiming a Vedic source.
